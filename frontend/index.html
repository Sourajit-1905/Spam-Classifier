Data Privacy

Your privacy is important to us. Here is exactly what this application does and does not do with your data:

  • No messages are stored. Your SMS text is processed in memory and immediately discarded after prediction.
  • No personal data is collected. We do not collect names, phone numbers, IP addresses, or any identifying information.
  • No third-party sharing. Your input never leaves the server — it is not sent to any external service or analytics platform.
  • No cookies or tracking. This application uses no cookies, local storage, or browser fingerprinting.
  • Open source. The full source code is publicly available on GitHub for independent verification.
